About Us

Two Decades of Hygiene Excellence

Cleanfellas CC is a certified Level 1 B-BBEE contributor and 100% black female owned company led by Faye Khan. Operational since 2004, we began as a trusted service provider to major hygiene companies including Nampak Tissue, Red Alert and Supercare Hygiene Ltd.

By choosing Cleanfellas, you contribute to a more inclusive economy and support the growth of a proudly South African, female-led business.

We specialise in low to high pH chemical deep cleaning and ultrasonic heat baths, treatments and cleaning. Our deep cleans eliminate major bacteria like E. coli and listeria — and we prove it with before-and-after swab tests.

Our staff are trained in installation, servicing and maintenance as well as workplace etiquette, and we work around your schedule — including after hours, weekends and public holidays.
